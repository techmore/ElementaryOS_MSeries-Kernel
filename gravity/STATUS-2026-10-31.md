The kernel `48be8a8` contains every topic branch, including `mini/wifi` (BCM4388 Wi-Fi calibration loaded from a file): `mini/hdmi` (dcpext0 + DPTX PHY), `mini/audio` (snd-t8132-speaker), `mini/usb4` (device tree, routers disabled), `mini/smc-power` (power button, reboot, poweroff) and `mini/bt` (BCM4388 Bluetooth calibration loaded from a file).

m1n1 `aac8189` adds:
- the J773g HDMI handoff (hands the running dcpext0 pipe to the kernel; fail-soft);
- `__OS_LOG` reservations for dcp, dcpext0 and dcpext1 on every J773g boot, so the running display coprocessors never write into Linux RAM;
- removal of the retired macos-14 CI job.
